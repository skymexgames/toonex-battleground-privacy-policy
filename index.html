Toonex BattleGround – Privacy Policy

By Skymex Games. Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

Toonex BattleGround (the "Game") is a toon-style online shooter made by Skymex Games ("we", "us", "our"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at contact.skymexgames@gmail.com.

2. Short version

We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. We do not run our own servers or databases for player information, and we do not use accounts, e-mail sign-ups or chat. The Game is played in your browser on the Poki platform, and some limited information is handled by Poki as described below.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress in your own browser storage (not on our servers) so it is still there when you come back. This can include:

  • your player name and a short random player tag (for example "Name#A7K2");
  • your coins, unlocked weapons, gun skins and selected loadout;
  • game settings such as sensitivity and quality.

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. These saves are not tracking cookies. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data for the Game. Please note that if you clear site data, play in private/incognito mode, or your browser blocks storage, your progress may not be saved.

4. Player name

When you play, you can create a player name. Choose a name that does not reveal personal information, such as your real name, address or contact details. Names pass through a filter that blocks inappropriate words. In online matches, your player name and tag are shown to the other players in the same match.

5. Multiplayer

Online matches use the Poki Networking Library (Netlib), provided by Poki. Netlib is used to create and find game lobbies and to connect players in the same match. To make this work, Poki's service handles the technical data needed to connect players, such as lobby information and network connection details. The Game has no text chat or voice chat. Skymex Games does not store any of this data.

6. Poki SDK, advertising and analytics

The Game is distributed through Poki and uses the Poki SDK. Poki shows the advertisements (including optional rewarded videos that give an in-game discount or bonus) and provides platform features. Poki and its partners may collect and process data such as your IP address, approximate location (country), device and browser information, and advertising identifiers, and may use cookies or similar technologies, in order to run the platform, show ads and measure performance.

Skymex Games does not control this processing. How Poki handles your data is described in Poki's own privacy policy, which applies to your use of the platform: https://poki.com/en/privacy

7. What we can see

We do not receive personal data about individual players. Through the Poki developer dashboard we can only see aggregated, non-identifying statistics, such as:

  • Netlib activity (for example, which game lobbies were created or are available);
  • the countries players come from;
  • basic device information (for example, desktop or mobile).

We use these statistics only to understand how the Game is used and to improve it.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not ask for your real name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • We do not use accounts or collect payment information. The Game has no real-money purchases.
  • We do not run our own advertising or tracking tools.

9. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience, including younger players, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the Game has no chat and filters player names, we try to keep online play safe. We encourage parents and guardians to remind children not to use personal information in their player name. If you believe a name or any content in the Game is inappropriate, please contact us.

10. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can control what is stored on your device by clearing your browser's site data. For data handled by Poki, such as ad and platform data, please use the options described in Poki's privacy policy. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under data protection laws, and you are welcome to contact us with any request or question at contact.skymexgames@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the Game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
